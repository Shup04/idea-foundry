## Known limitations

The evidence prototype depends on honest manual source attribution and human review of paraphrases; hashes do not prove authorship, relevance or truth. Canonical keys/stances and explicit edges handle known corrections and contradictions, but differently keyed paraphrases need review. Reported project history does not demonstrate skill or establish current activity. Model-authored estimates remain assumptions, and qualitative fit bands do not imply strong evidence. Outcome learning has one inspectable proposal rule demonstrated only with separate synthetic history. Exclusion removes information from active evaluation, not from the retained audit history. No live provider integration, external research, ingestion, full application or real outcome experiment was performed for this prototype.

The original shared generation context and unequal effort prevent an independent comparison. The revision does not rerun that comparison. Three public checks establish what their primary documentation describes; no proposed product or next test was executed. Structural validity and source-reference resolution do not prove truth or usefulness. Novelty, market demand and user comprehension remain unmeasured. Usage and cost are unknown. No gate has been approved, and no later pass has begun.
